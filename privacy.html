Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Who uses it

This is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Gareth Campbell, who signs in with his own Google account.

What it accesses

It requests one Google permission, youtube.upload, and uses it only to upload videos the owner has made to the owner's own channel, as private videos. It does not read, collect or store any other Google or YouTube data.

What it stores

The sign-in token is kept privately by the owner, in his own computer and private cloud settings, so the daily upload can run. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

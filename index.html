Claude Motion uploader

A private script that uploads one short explainer video a day, made by its owner, to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It is used only by its owner, Gareth Campbell. It asks for one permission, to upload videos to YouTube, and every upload is set to private. It reads no other data, and it is not offered to anyone else.
